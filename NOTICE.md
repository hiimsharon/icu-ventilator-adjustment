# Research and Use Notice

## Project Scope

This repository presents research and portfolio materials from a
master's research project on ICU mechanical ventilator adjustment.

The research includes:

- clinical ventilator data preprocessing;
- multivariate time-series construction;
- LSTM and GRU modeling;
- Bayesian hyperparameter optimization using the
  Tree-structured Parzen Estimator;
- repeated data splitting and model evaluation;
- ventilator adjustment requirement determination;
- mode adjustment direction classification; and
- parameter adjustment prediction.

## Data Availability

Clinical source data are not included in this repository.

Patient-level data, identifiable information, private institutional
records, credentials, restricted clinical datasets, and
confidential materials are not publicly distributed.

## Research Prototype

Any interface displayed in this repository is a research prototype
created to demonstrate the presentation of the proposed decision
workflow.

It is not a deployed clinical system, certified medical device, or
clinically validated decision-support product.

## Clinical Disclaimer

Repository contents must not be used to:

- make clinical decisions;
- diagnose or treat a patient;
- determine real-world ventilator settings;
- replace professional medical judgment; or
- support patient care without independent validation,
  institutional approval, clinical governance, and applicable
  regulatory review.
